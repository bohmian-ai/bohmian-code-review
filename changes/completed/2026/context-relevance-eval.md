# Context relevance evaluation

Completed 2026-09-28. Reviewed target: `058e3d13dac3412caa39d30b9094a1622b22cd3c`. Delivery reference: not supplied.

This change measured whether the review-context pipeline ranks files co-changed in real merged PRs, and compared three cumulative, evaluation-only ranking variants with its production baseline. The read-only `live:context-relevance` command samples 50 PRs each from `pydantic/pydantic-ai` and `tokio-rs/tokio` before `2026-09-01T00:00:00Z`, runs leave-one-out trials against complete fixed-commit base snapshots, and emits source-free JSON. The checked-in reports contain 171 Python and 159 Rust trials, per-variant ranks and packing outcomes, all/non-test aggregates, and every miss.

Production ranking, packing, extraction, snapshot and PR-context behavior, persisted formats, public APIs, and the normal gate remain unchanged. The evaluator uses verified provider blobs in memory, the public symbolic facade for baseline behavior, and one common 100,000-token experimental candidate allowance. Its results provide evidence for a later, separately approved production decision; they set no pass threshold.

The approved specification reached revision 4 to fix the sample failure/omission boundary, full base candidate universe, and import-scoping rule. The human owner accepted two deviations after task review: patches parsing to no hunks may be absent from the omission list, and old-side hunk coordinates are not validated against the verified base blob. These limits are confined to the opt-in evaluation and remain visible in the final review record.

Acceptance evidence: the sampler and trial tests prove qualification, withholding, variant scoring, baseline equality and packing outcomes; the checked-in reports prove both 50-PR samples and required measurements; two recorded live runs had byte-identical output; TASK-003 passed independent task review; and the final change review passed the pipeline tests, full gate and diff check. The second raw live stdout was not retained, so reproducibility beyond its recorded comparison is not independently replayable from that artifact.
